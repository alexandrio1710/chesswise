What profiles are

No accounts, no login — a profile is just a name plus the Lichess/Chess.com usernames linked to it, so more than one person's games (or your own separate accounts) can share this local database. A username can only be linked to one profile at a time; fetching for a brand-new username auto-creates a profile for it. The profile switcher on Dashboard, Insights, Clock, and Search narrows those pages to one profile's games — Puzzles, the Opening Explorer, the Endgame Trainer, and the Analyze board always use the full local game pool.
